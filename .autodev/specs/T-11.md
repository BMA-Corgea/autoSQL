# T-11 — Promote the compiler, and route its numbers through `xpr.j`

**Spawned by:** T-9 · **Authority:** GA-14 (*"keep going with T-11"*) · **Pipeline:** `feature@v1` ·
**Lean OFF, risk HIGH** — this changes the SQL every dashboard query is made of.

## 1. The gap

T-9 added `xpr.j(float8) → jsonb`, immune to `extra_float_digits` because it carries its own
`SET`. **Nothing generated calls to it**, so the immunity reached nothing.

The demo's queries emit bare `to_jsonb(<float8>)` at the projection, and that emission comes from
`spikes/T-1/proto/compile.py`, which `demo/builder.py` loaded **directly**. That file is **frozen
evidence** — sha `b71b153802d0df94…`, cited in T-6's attestation and all 42 battery outputs — so the
one line that needed fixing could not be fixed where it lived.

## 2. Scope

- **`compiler/compile.py`** — the shipping compiler, promoted from the spike. The spike copy stays
  byte-identical forever.
- **Float8-valued emissions routed through `xpr.j`** (18 sites). Text and boolean stay on
  `to_jsonb`: neither has digits to lose.
- **`demo/builder.py`** loads `compiler/`, not the spike.
- **`compiler/tests/`** — including the equivalence test and the frozen-compiler control.

## 3. Out of scope

- The runtime (T-8/T-9's, unchanged) · GIMS (Q3 park) · the frozen spike artifacts · anything about
  what the demo *shows* (T-8 settled that).

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** — the shipping compiler's output is **identical at efd 1, 0 and −3**. *Test:* three
  connections, one statement.
- **AC-2** — the **frozen** compiler's output still **moves**. *Test:* the same, as the control —
  without it AC-1 could pass on a platform that protected everything, and prove nothing.
- **AC-3** — compiling any expression with both modules differs by **exactly** the
  `to_jsonb → xpr.j` swap and nothing else. *Test:* 24 expressions, byte-compared after undoing the
  swap.
- **AC-4** — text and boolean results are **not** wrapped. *Test:* asserted per construct.
- **AC-5** — `spikes/T-1/proto/compile.py` still hashes to `b71b153802d0df94`. *Test:* sha256.
- **AC-6** — the demo loads the shipping compiler. *Test:* source assertion.
- **AC-7** — the demo suite stays green. *Test:* `./run-demo test`.

## 5. What would make this unacceptable

- Editing the frozen spike compiler or runtime.
- Wrapping text/boolean results "for consistency".
- An immunity claim without its control.
- Leaving the demo red, or green by weakening an assertion.
