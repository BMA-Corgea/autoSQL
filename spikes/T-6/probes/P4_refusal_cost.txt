Of 86 coercion refusals at the pinned setting, 26 (30%) replaced a SILENT WRONG
NUMBER -- a straight win -- and 60 (70%) replaced an answer that was already
CORRECT, because the bad NULL was absorbed downstream and never reached the result.

This is inherent to the design, not a defect in it: xpr.num refuses where the
coercion happens, and at that point nothing knows whether the value will matter.
Refusing only values that change the answer is not expressible in the compiled SQL.
